---
name: accessibility-check
description: Check that an interface works for everyone. Use when building or reviewing UI.
---

- Every control works with the keyboard alone, in a sensible order, with a visible focus.
- Text contrast is at least 4.5:1; state is never shown by colour alone.
- Images have text alternatives; icons that act have labels.
- Forms: every field has a label, errors say what to fix and where.
- The page works at 400px wide and at 200% zoom without sideways scrolling.
- Motion respects the reduced-motion setting.
