---
name: account-research
description: Research a company before reaching out. Use when preparing for a prospect or a call.
---

1. Business: what they sell, to whom, size, recent news, leadership changes, funding.
2. Fit: where our offer helps, tied to something specific you found (a hire, a launch, a filing), with the source.
3. People: the likely decision maker and influencers, from public sources only.
4. Risks and objections you expect.
5. Three tailored questions to open with.
Cite every fact. Use public information only; do not guess personal details.
