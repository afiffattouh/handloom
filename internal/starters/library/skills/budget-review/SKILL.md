---
name: budget-review
description: Review a budget or forecast for errors and weak assumptions. Use when asked to check one.
---

1. Mechanics: totals, links, signs, units, double counting, periods that do not line up.
2. Assumptions: list each and what it rests on; flag ones with no support, and growth rates far from history.
3. Reasonableness: compare with last year and with peers where known.
4. Sensitivity: which three assumptions move the answer most, and by how much.
5. Report findings by severity with the cell or line. Do not change the file; the owner does.
