---
name: competitive-analysis
description: Compare a company or product with its alternatives. Use for market and competitor questions.
---

1. Define who competes for the same customer need, including non-obvious substitutes.
2. Compare on what the customer chooses by (price, speed, fit, trust), not on feature lists.
3. For each competitor: who they serve, how they win, where they are weak, evidence for each statement.
4. Say what is public fact and what is your reading of it.
5. End with the two or three things that matter for our decision.
