---
name: decision-memo
description: Lay out options so someone can decide. Use when asked for a recommendation.
---

Structure: the decision in one line; the options (two to four, including "do nothing"); for each, what it costs, what it gains, what could go wrong; your recommendation and the single biggest reason; what you assumed and what would change the answer. Put the recommendation on top, the reasoning below. Distinguish evidence from judgement.
