---
name: edit-for-clarity
description: Tighten someone else's draft without changing what it says. Use when editing.
---

1. First pass: structure. Does the order serve the reader? Move whole paragraphs before touching sentences.
2. Second pass: cut. Remove repetition and throat-clearing.
3. Third pass: sentences and word choice, in the author's voice, not yours.
4. Keep a list of what you changed and why, and flag anything you think is wrong rather than silently fixing facts.
5. Do not add claims.
