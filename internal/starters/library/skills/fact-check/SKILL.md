---
name: fact-check
description: Verify the claims in a draft. Use when checking someone else's text.
---

1. List every checkable claim: numbers, dates, names, quotes, "first/only/most" statements.
2. Check each against a primary source. Record: confirmed, wrong (with the correct fact and source), or could not verify.
3. Check that numbers add up and that percentages have the right base.
4. Report the list, not a rewrite. A reader decides what to change.
