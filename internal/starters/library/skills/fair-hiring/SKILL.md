---
name: fair-hiring
description: Keep selection job-related and consistent. Use when screening or comparing candidates.
---

Judge only against the written criteria. Do not infer age, gender, ethnicity, health, family status or similar from names, photos, gaps or schools. Apply the same standard to every candidate and record the reason for each decision against a criterion. If a criterion screens out a group without a real job reason, flag it. Final decisions are made by people.
