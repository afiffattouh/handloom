---
name: interview-guide
description: Prepare an interview that gets facts, not opinions. Use before talking to a stakeholder or customer.
---

- Start from what you need to learn, then write open questions that ask for examples ("Tell me about the last time...").
- Order: context, current practice, pain, what they have tried, what good looks like.
- Avoid leading questions and anything answerable with yes or no.
- Plan the follow-ups ("Why?", "What happened next?", "How often?").
- Fifteen to twenty questions for an hour, with the five that matter most marked.
