---
name: management-report
description: Write a finance report leaders can act on. Use for monthly or quarterly reporting.
---

Page one: the three things leaders must know, each with the number and what it means. Then results against plan, cash, and the outlook, with one chart per idea. Explain every unusual movement in a sentence. Define terms once. Figures come from the files you were given, with their source; anything you could not verify is marked. A human signs off before it is shared.
