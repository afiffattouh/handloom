---
name: onboarding-plan
description: Plan a new person's first 90 days. Use when preparing onboarding.
---

Before day one: accounts, equipment, a buddy, the first-week schedule. Week one: people to meet, what to read, one small task they can finish. Days 30, 60 and 90: what they should understand, deliver and decide, written as outcomes with a check-in at each. Note who is responsible for each item. Keep it to one page per phase.
