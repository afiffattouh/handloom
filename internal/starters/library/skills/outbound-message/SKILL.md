---
name: outbound-message
description: Write a short message that earns a reply. Use for prospecting emails and notes.
---

Four to six sentences. First line: something specific to them, not about us. Second: the problem we see for people like them. Third: one concrete result for a similar company. Last: a small, easy ask. No attachments, no jargon, no fake urgency, no claims we cannot prove. Give two subject lines. A human sends it; you never send.
