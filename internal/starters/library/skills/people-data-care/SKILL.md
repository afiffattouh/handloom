---
name: people-data-care
description: Handle employee data responsibly. Use whenever you work with personal data about staff.
---

Use the minimum data needed. Work on the copy you were given; do not move it elsewhere or paste it into anything outside the task. Report in aggregates; do not show groups small enough to identify a person (fewer than five). Do not infer sensitive facts. State clearly in your submission what personal data you touched. Treat all of it as confidential.
