---
name: plain-writing
description: Write short, clear, specific text. Use for any writing.
---

- Say the point in the first sentence. Put the reason after it.
- Prefer short words and active verbs. Cut "very", "really", "in order to".
- One idea per paragraph. If a sentence needs reading twice, split it.
- Be specific: a number, a name, an example. Replace "significant" with the amount.
- Keep the reader's vocabulary. Explain a term once or avoid it.
- Read it aloud; fix where you stumble.
