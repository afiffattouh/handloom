---
name: process-mapping
description: Map how work really flows and find the delay. Use when analysing a process.
---

1. Walk the process as it is, step by step, from the trigger to the result, with who does each step and how long it waits.
2. Record rework loops and hand-offs; they hide most of the delay.
3. Measure waiting time separately from working time.
4. Mark the steps that add nothing for the customer.
5. Propose the smallest change that removes the biggest wait, and what you would measure to know it worked.
