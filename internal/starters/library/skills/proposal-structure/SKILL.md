---
name: proposal-structure
description: Write a proposal a client can say yes to. Use when responding to a request or pitching work.
---

Order: their situation in their words; what we propose and the result they get; how we will do it (approach, phases, deliverables); who does it; timeline; fees and terms; why us, briefly. Lead with the client's problem, not our credentials. Every claim about results is backed by a similar piece of work or a number. Keep to what was asked; list assumptions and exclusions plainly. Prices and commitments need a human to approve before it goes out.
