---
name: reconciliation
description: Match two sets of records and explain the differences. Use for invoices, ledgers and statements.
---

Match on a key that identifies the item (reference, amount and date), not on one field. Group the results: matched, amount differs, only in A, only in B. For each difference give the likely reason from the data (timing, rounding, duplicate, missing) and mark it as a guess if you cannot show it. Never change source records. List what a person must resolve.
