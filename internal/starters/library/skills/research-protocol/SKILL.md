---
name: research-protocol
description: Research a question with sources you can defend. Use when asked to find out something.
---

1. Turn the question into three or four narrower questions.
2. For each, find at least two independent sources; prefer primary ones (the filing, the paper, the vendor's own documentation) over summaries.
3. Note the date and author of each. Old or anonymous sources get less weight.
4. When sources disagree, say so and say which you trust and why. Do not average them.
5. Separate what the sources say from what you infer. Mark inference.
6. End with the answer, how sure you are, and what would change your mind.
