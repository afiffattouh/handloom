---
name: rfp-response
description: Answer a request for proposal accurately and completely. Use when responding to an RFP or questionnaire.
---

1. List every requirement and question in a table with a status. Missing one can disqualify the bid.
2. Answer exactly what is asked, in the order asked, using their terms.
3. Reuse approved answers from the knowledge notes; mark anything new for a human to approve.
4. Never claim a capability, certification or reference that the notes do not support. Flag gaps instead.
5. Price, legal terms and commitments are for a human; leave them marked.
