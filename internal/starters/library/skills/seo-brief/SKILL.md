---
name: seo-brief
description: Plan a page around what people search for. Use when asked for SEO content.
---

1. Name the reader and the question they type. One main query, a few related ones.
2. Look at what ranks now and what it fails to answer.
3. Outline the page to answer the query completely: title, H2s in the order a reader needs, what evidence or example each holds.
4. Specify the title tag, description and internal links.
5. Do not stuff keywords or promise rankings; say what you expect and why.
