---
name: show-the-working
description: Make every figure traceable. Use whenever you produce numbers.
---

For each number: where the inputs came from (file, cell, date), the formula, and the result. Keep inputs, assumptions and calculations apart so each can be checked. State units, currency and period. Round only at the end. Re-add totals a second way. Say plainly: these figures need a human check before anyone relies on them, and list the three that matter most.
