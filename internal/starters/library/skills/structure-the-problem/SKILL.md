---
name: structure-the-problem
description: Break a business problem into parts that do not overlap and cover everything. Use at the start of an engagement or analysis.
---

1. State the question as a decision someone must make ("Should we enter market X?"), not a topic.
2. Build a tree: split the question into parts that are mutually exclusive and together complete (profit = revenue minus cost; revenue = customers times price).
3. Prioritise the branches that could change the answer; park the rest.
4. For each priority branch write a hypothesis you can prove or disprove, and the data that would do it.
5. Share the tree before doing the work. A wrong structure wastes the whole analysis.
