---
name: structured-interview
description: Design interviews that compare candidates fairly. Use when preparing hiring interviews.
---

1. Define the three to five things the job needs, from the job description and the work itself.
2. For each, write one or two questions about past behaviour or a work sample, and a scoring guide with what weak, fine and strong answers look like.
3. Ask every candidate the same questions in the same order.
4. Score each answer on its own before discussing.
5. Keep questions to the job. Never ask about protected characteristics, family plans, health or age.
