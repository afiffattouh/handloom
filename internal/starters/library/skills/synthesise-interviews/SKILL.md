---
name: synthesise-interviews
description: Turn interview notes into findings. Use after several interviews.
---

1. Code each note: what they did, what hurt, what they wanted, in their words.
2. Group by theme. Count how many people raised each, and say how many you spoke to.
3. Separate what people said from what you conclude. Keep one telling quote per theme.
4. Note disagreements and outliers; do not smooth them away.
5. Report themes ranked by how many people and how strongly, then implications. Remove names unless the person agreed.
