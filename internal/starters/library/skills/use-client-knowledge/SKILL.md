---
name: use-client-knowledge
description: Read and extend the client's knowledge notes. Use when the folder .handloom/knowledge exists.
---

1. Before you plan or start, read the notes that concern your task. Search the folder first; do not read everything.
2. Treat the notes as the team's record, but check anything that looks out of date against what you can see.
3. If you learn something the next person needs (a decision, a preference, a fact you had to find out), add one short note: a clear file name, a title, the fact, and where it came from.
4. Edit an existing note only when it is wrong, and say why in the note. Your changes are proposals: a human reviews them.
5. Put anything private to the client only under `confidential/`, never elsewhere.
