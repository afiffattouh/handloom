---
name: write-a-handoff
description: What to put in a submission so the reviewer can judge it fast. Use whenever you submit a task.
---

- Evidence: the thing itself (`commit:<sha>`, `file:<path>`), not a description of it.
- One note of at most five lines: what you did, what you chose not to do and why, what you could not verify.
- Name anything you are unsure about. A reviewer forgives an honest "not checked" and never forgives a hidden one.
- Do not claim a test passed unless you ran it; paste the command and its result.
