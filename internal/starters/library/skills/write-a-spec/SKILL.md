---
name: write-a-spec
description: Turn a goal into a spec an engineer can build. Use when writing requirements.
---

1. Problem: who has it, how they cope today, what it costs them. One paragraph.
2. Goal and non-goals. Be explicit about what you are not doing.
3. Behaviour as numbered, testable statements ("When X, the system does Y").
4. Acceptance criteria a check could verify.
5. Open questions, each with who can answer it. Do not hide a decision inside the spec.
6. Keep it to what changes behaviour; leave implementation to the builder unless a constraint demands it.
